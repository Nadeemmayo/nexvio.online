Our story

Why we started Nexvio

Nexvio was founded by Nadeem Mayo after years of delivering SEO and link building behind the scenes for agencies in the UK and US. Too often he saw brands paying for reports full of rankings that never turned into revenue.

Nexvio exists to do the opposite: connect every piece of SEO, PR, paid media and web work to the numbers that matter to a growing business, and report on them honestly.

Today we focus on UK SaaS, e-commerce and tech brands, and we increasingly help them put AI to work in their marketing and customer service.
